Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

Effective Date: September 2026 • Scope: In-House Production System • Single-Tenant Private Architecture

Introduction & Personal-Use Scope

This Privacy Policy and Terms of Service document governs the operation of the OhhoChadchat Uploader (Content Delivery & Video Upload Automation System). This software is an in-house desktop automation utility engineered strictly for private editorial and channel publishing operations for the verified creator channel Ohho Chadchat.

Note for Platform Compliance Officers: This system is strictly for personal, in-house administration of our verified channels. It is NOT a public SaaS platform, multi-tenant service, or commercial product offered to third parties.

1. Terms of Service & Non-Commercial Use

The OhhoChadchat Uploader is proprietary software operated exclusively by the internal production team of Ohho Chadchat:

  • No Public Registration: There are no public user accounts, registrations, subscriptions, or fees.
  • Exclusive Channel Management: All uploaded videos, titles, and descriptions are proprietary creator assets owned by Ohho Chadchat.
  • Platform Terms Adherence: The application complies with TikTok Developer Terms, Meta Platform Terms, and YouTube Developer Policies.

2. Data Collection and OAuth Scopes Usage

The software requests explicit OAuth permissions from the channel administrator to perform automated video publishing:

user.info.basic TikTok API • Verify identity of authenticated channel
video.upload TikTok API • Upload video directly to the creator's drafts or feed.
video.publish TikTok API • Publish scheduled video clips to creator feed

Local Offline Token Storage: All OAuth tokens (Access Tokens and Refresh Tokens) are encrypted and stored 100% locally strictly on Ohho Chadchat's internal production workstations. At no point are tokens or credentials transmitted to an external web server, cloud database, or third-party relay.

3. Data Sharing & Retention

We maintain an uncompromising privacy standard:

  • Zero Data Brokering: We do not sell, trade, rent, or share user data, channel analytics, or video files with any third party.
  • Authorized Official Endpoints: Videos and metadata are sent directly and securely to the official API endpoints of Google, Meta, and TikTok.
  • Immediate Revocation: Deleting the local configuration folder or revoking access via platform security settings immediately destroys all local session states.
