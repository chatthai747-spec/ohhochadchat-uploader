Privacy Policy & Terms of Service

OhhoChadchat Uploader - Personal Use Only

Last updated: September 2026

This software is a private, non-commercial automation tool designed solely for personal workflow management and uploading authorized media content to TikTok.

1. Terms of Service & Non-Commercial Use

This application is developed strictly for internal and personal use. It is not distributed, sold, or offered as a public SaaS service. The user maintains full ownership and responsibility for their TikTok credentials and uploaded media.

2. Data Collection and OAuth Usage

  • user.info.basic: Used solely to display the connected TikTok profile name on the desktop interface.
  • video.upload & video.publish: Used exclusively to upload and publish user-selected videos directly to TikTok.
  • Authentication Tokens: Stored strictly on the user's local machine. No external database, remote server, or tracking services are used.

3. Data Sharing & Retention

We do not share, sell, rent, or distribute any user data, tokens, or video files to third parties. Deleting local application files permanently erases all session credentials.

4. Contact

For any questions regarding this utility, please contact via the GitHub repository.
